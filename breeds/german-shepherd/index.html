Living with a German Shepherd

They generally need substantial daily activity, structured training, and mental work. Their adult size and strength make early manners important.

Grooming and routine care

Their double coat sheds and benefits from regular brushing, especially through seasonal coat changes.

Training and socialization

Early socialization and consistent reward-based training are important. They often thrive when given clear expectations and meaningful work.

Before choosing a German Shepherd puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and available records. Breed descriptions help narrow a search but do not guarantee one puppy's adult size, coat, health, or personality.
