Living with a Boxer

They need daily activity, play, and training. Their enthusiasm and size make early manners important.

Grooming and routine care

The short coat is low maintenance, with routine nail, ear, dental, and skin care.

Training and socialization

Boxers often respond well to upbeat, consistent training that channels energy into clear tasks and household expectations.

Before choosing a puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and available records. Breed descriptions help narrow a search, but they do not guarantee adult size, coat, health, or personality.

Frequently asked questions

How big do Boxer dogs typically get?

Boxer dogs are generally large, though individual adult size can vary.

How active are Boxer dogs?

Typical activity needs are high. The specific puppy's age and personality matter too.
