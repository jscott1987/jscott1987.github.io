Living with a Morkie

They need limited physical space but still benefit from regular play, short walks, and social interaction.

Grooming and routine care

Fine, longer coats require regular brushing and grooming to stay manageable.

Training and socialization

Small-dog basics matter: consistent house training, safe socialization, and clear routines help prevent behavior problems from being overlooked because of size.

Before choosing a Morkie puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and what records are provided. Breed descriptions are useful for narrowing your search, but they are not a guarantee of any one puppy's adult size, coat, health, or personality.

Frequently asked questions

How big do Morkie dogs typically get?

Morkie dogs are generally toy, though individual adult size can vary.

How active are Morkie dogs?

Typical activity needs are moderate. The specific puppy's personality and age matter just as much as the breed label.
