Breed profile

What it’s like to live with a Australian Shepherd.

Breed descriptions help narrow the search. Individual puppies can still vary in adult size, coat, health, and personality.

Grooming and routine care

Their medium double coat needs regular brushing and seasonal shedding management.

Training and socialization

Aussies often learn quickly and can excel at advanced training, but they need appropriate outlets for herding drive and energy.

Before choosing a puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and available records. Breed descriptions help narrow a search, but they do not guarantee adult size, coat, health, or personality.
