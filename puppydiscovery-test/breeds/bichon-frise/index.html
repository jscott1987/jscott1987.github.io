Living with a Bichon Frise

They often suit smaller homes while still needing daily play, walks, and social interaction.

Grooming and routine care

The coat requires frequent brushing and professional grooming to stay clean and free of mats.

Training and socialization

Consistent house training and early socialization are important. Bichons often respond well to positive reinforcement.

Before choosing a puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and available records. Breed descriptions help narrow a search, but they do not guarantee adult size, coat, health, or personality.

Frequently asked questions

How big do Bichon Frise dogs typically get?

Bichon Frise dogs are generally small, though individual adult size can vary.

How active are Bichon Frise dogs?

Typical activity needs are moderate. The specific puppy's age and personality matter too.
