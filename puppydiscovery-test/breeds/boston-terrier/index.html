Living with a Boston Terrier

They often adapt well to city or suburban homes with daily walks, play, and interaction.

Grooming and routine care

Their short coat is relatively easy to maintain, though routine nail, ear, dental, and skin care remain important.

Training and socialization

Boston Terriers are often responsive to positive training and enjoy short sessions mixed with play.

Before choosing a Boston Terrier puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and what records are provided. Breed descriptions are useful for narrowing your search, but they are not a guarantee of any one puppy's adult size, coat, health, or personality.

Frequently asked questions

How big do Boston Terrier dogs typically get?

Boston Terrier dogs are generally small, though individual adult size can vary.

How active are Boston Terrier dogs?

Typical activity needs are moderate. The specific puppy's personality and age matter just as much as the breed label.
