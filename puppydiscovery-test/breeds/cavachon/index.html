Living with a Cavachon

They often suit households looking for a smaller dog that still enjoys walks, play, and regular interaction.

Grooming and routine care

Coats can be wavy or curly and usually require brushing plus routine professional grooming.

Training and socialization

Early socialization and positive training help build comfort with grooming, visitors, and everyday household routines.

Before choosing a Cavachon puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and what records are provided. Breed descriptions are useful for narrowing your search, but they are not a guarantee of any one puppy's adult size, coat, health, or personality.

How big do Cavachon dogs typically get?

Cavachon dogs are generally small, though individual adult size can vary.
