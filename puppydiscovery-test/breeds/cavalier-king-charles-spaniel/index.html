Living with a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel

They usually enjoy daily walks and play but are also comfortable spending substantial time with the household.

Grooming and routine care

Silky coats need regular brushing, especially around ears, feathering, and friction points.

Training and socialization

They are often responsive to gentle, reward-based training and early socialization.

Before choosing a puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and available records. Breed descriptions help narrow a search, but they do not guarantee adult size, coat, health, or personality.

How big do Cavalier King Charles Spaniel dogs typically get?
