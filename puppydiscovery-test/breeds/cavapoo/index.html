01 / Daily life

Small footprint, social rhythm.

Cavapoos are generally small to medium dogs. Many enjoy being close to their people and benefit from a consistent daily routine.

02 / Activity

Play, walks, and engagement.

Plan for daily activity and interaction. Energy levels vary, so ask about the individual puppy rather than relying only on breed averages.

03 / Grooming

A coat that needs a plan.

Coat texture varies. Regular brushing and professional grooming may be needed to keep the coat comfortable and manageable.

04 / Training

Consistency matters more than cleverness.

Positive, consistent training and early socialization can help a puppy build comfortable household habits.

03 / Before bringing one home

Know what to ask.

01

02

03

04
