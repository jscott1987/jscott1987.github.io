Living with a Cavapoo

Cavapoos usually do best with regular interaction, daily play, and a predictable routine. Their size can work well in apartments or houses when exercise and attention needs are met.

Grooming and routine care

Coat texture can range from wavy to curlier. Regular brushing and professional grooming are commonly part of the routine.

Training and socialization

Cavapoos are often responsive to positive, consistent training. Early socialization helps them become comfortable with visitors, handling, travel, and new environments.

Before choosing a Cavapoo puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and available records. Breed descriptions help narrow a search, but they do not guarantee adult size, coat, health, or personality.

Frequently asked questions

How big do Cavapoos typically get?

Cavapoos are generally small to medium dogs, though adult size can vary considerably.

How active are Cavapoos?

Most have moderate activity needs and benefit from daily walks, play, and interaction.
