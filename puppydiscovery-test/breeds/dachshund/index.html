Living with a Dachshund

Their small size works in many homes, but they still need walks, play, and mental stimulation. Their long back also makes thoughtful handling and household setup important.

Grooming and routine care

Coat care depends on whether the dog is smooth, longhaired, or wirehaired. Routine nails, ears, dental care, and weight management remain important.

Training and socialization

They can be independent and scent-driven. Short positive sessions and consistent routines tend to work better than repetitive drilling.

Before choosing a Dachshund puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and available records. Breed descriptions help narrow a search but do not guarantee one puppy's adult size, coat, health, or personality.
