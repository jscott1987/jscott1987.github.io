Double Doodle Puppies

Double Doodles are multi-breed doodle crosses commonly combining Golden Retriever, Labrador Retriever, and Poodle lineage, though exact parentage can vary.

Living with a Double Doodle

Adult size and energy can vary substantially. Most benefit from regular exercise, interaction, and mental stimulation.

Grooming and routine care

Wavy or curly coats can require frequent brushing and routine professional grooming.

Training and socialization

Many are people-oriented and responsive to positive training, but individual temperament depends on the specific parents and puppy.

Before choosing a Double Doodle puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and available records. Breed descriptions help narrow a search but do not guarantee one puppy's adult size, coat, health, or personality.
