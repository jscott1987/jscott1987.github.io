Living with a Pomsky

They are often more active than their size suggests and benefit from structured exercise, play, and mental stimulation.

Grooming and routine care

The thick coat can shed heavily and requires consistent brushing.

Training and socialization

Pomskies can combine intelligence with independence. Early training, engagement, and secure management are important.

Before choosing a Pomsky puppy

Ask about the individual puppy's age, current routine, veterinary information, feeding schedule, temperament observations, and what records are provided. Breed descriptions are useful for narrowing your search, but they are not a guarantee of any one puppy's adult size, coat, health, or personality.

Frequently asked questions

How big do Pomsky dogs typically get?

Pomsky dogs are generally small to medium, though individual adult size can vary.

How active are Pomsky dogs?

Typical activity needs are high. The specific puppy's personality and age matter just as much as the breed label.
