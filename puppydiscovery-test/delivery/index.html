What to confirm before making plans

Pickup and delivery arrangements should be confirmed directly for the individual puppy. Do not assume a transport option is available until the store confirms it.

Ask about

  • Whether pickup in Stuart is required or another option is available.
  • Timing and any location restrictions.
  • What records and puppy information travel with the puppy.
  • The exact next step for the puppy you are interested in.
