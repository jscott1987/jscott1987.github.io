Questions to ask about a puppy

Before you make travel plans or commit to a puppy, get clear answers about the individual puppy, the records available, and what happens next.

Ask about the individual puppy

  • Current age and date of birth
  • Sex and identifying reference number
  • Current feeding and sleep routine
  • Temperament observations and handling
  • Anything the staff has noticed that is specific to this puppy

Ask what records are included

Find out what veterinary, vaccination, breeder, microchip, or other records are available and when you receive them.

Ask about health and veterinary follow-up

Get the current written terms rather than relying on a verbal summary. Ask what exam or follow-up is expected after bringing the puppy home and which provider must be used for any warranty coverage.

Confirm availability before traveling

A puppy can be listed when you start your search and unavailable by the time you drive to the store. Call before you travel if you are coming for a specific puppy.

Understand the total first-week setup

Food, crate or containment, leash and collar, grooming supplies, cleaning supplies, toys, and veterinary care are part of the first-week plan too.
