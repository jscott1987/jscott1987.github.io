Privacy Notice

Last updated October 2, 2026.

Information you choose to provide

PuppyDiscovery may collect contact details, puppy preferences, timing, and messages when you intentionally submit a request. In the current preview, the Find My Puppy flow prepares a text message that you choose whether to send.

Technical and analytics information

When analytics or first-party event tracking is enabled in production, PuppyDiscovery may record page views, referral information, campaign parameters, device/browser information, and conversion actions such as phone clicks or outbound website clicks. The production implementation should avoid fingerprinting and collect only what is needed to understand acquisition performance.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to puppy inquiries, route requests to the inventory partner, measure site performance, improve the search experience, prevent abuse, and maintain system security.

Third-party destinations

PuppyDiscovery links to The Noble Paw, maps, phone, and messaging services. Their privacy practices apply after you leave PuppyDiscovery or choose to communicate through those services.

Contact

For questions about a puppy, call The Noble Paw at 772-348-0800. This notice should be reviewed again when production analytics, email delivery, or database storage is activated.
